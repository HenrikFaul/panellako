# Marketing Value: Assembly Protocol Generator (v0.5.2)

**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Feature:** Közgyűlési Segéd — Assembly Protocol Generator
**Initiative:** #9 (final of 10)

## User-Language Problem Solved

Organizing a condo general assembly in Hungary means hours of paperwork after the meeting: manually typing up minutes, tracking who attended and what percentage of ownership that represents, recording each resolution and its vote outcome, then formatting everything according to Ptk. 5:84–5:88 requirements. Most building managers either delay this or produce inconsistent documents that don't hold up legally.

PanelLakó now handles the entire post-assembly workflow digitally: tap to mark who arrived, record resolutions as they're passed, and generate a legally-structured PDF minutes document with one click — automatically calculating quorum, listing every attendee and their ownership share, and structuring each agenda item with its resolution outcome.

## Personas Who Benefit

- **Közös képviselő (Building Manager):** Eliminates hours of post-meeting paperwork; generates compliant minutes instantly; no more "where did I put the attendance sheet" problems.
- **Tulajdonos (Owner/Resident):** Transparent record of what was decided and whether the meeting was legally quorate; downloadable PDF for personal records.
- **Könyvelő (Accountant):** Authoritative resolution record for financial decisions (special levies, repair fund allocations) that need to be implemented.
