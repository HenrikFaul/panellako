# Marketing Value: Közösségi Zöld Akciók (v0.9.4)

**Date:** 2026-05-22
**Feature:** Community Green Actions — `/w/[buildingId]/zold-akciok`
**Personas:** Environment-conscious residents, building managers, HOA board members

---

## User-Language Problem Solved

Residents who want to reduce their carbon footprint have no shared tool to measure, track, or coordinate green actions within their building community. They wonder: "How much CO₂ do I actually save by taking the tram instead of driving?" and "Would my neighbors support installing a covered bike storage?"

## What Was Built

A self-contained green actions hub that gives every building community three tools in one page:
1. **CO₂ savings calculator** — instantly shows how much carbon each transport choice saves vs. driving
2. **Personal transport journal** — persists trip history in the browser so residents can track their monthly CO₂ impact without registering data anywhere
3. **Building-level action voting** — 6 concrete green initiatives residents can champion, with a lightweight voting mechanic to gauge community interest

## Personas Who Benefit

- **Environmentally motivated residents** ("Zöld Hajnalka" archetype): gets a tangible, gamified sense of their own impact
- **Building manager / HOA chairperson**: can use the voting section to quickly gauge which green investment residents would support (bike storage, green wall, rainwater collection)
- **Commuters and cyclists**: the transport log doubles as a lightweight habit tracker

## Marketing Claims Enabled

- "Track your CO₂ savings trip by trip — right inside your building app"
- "See which green projects your neighbours want most — without a meeting"
- "A personal carbon diary that lives in your browser, no sign-up required"
- "From bike parking to rainwater collection — your building's green wishlist, all in one place"

## Content Angles

- **Social proof / community angle:** "Your building, greener together" — shows collective intent via the voting cards
- **Personal empowerment angle:** The calculator reframes everyday commute choices in grams of CO₂ — concretely motivating
- **Privacy angle:** trip log is browser-local; no data sent to any server — trust message for privacy-conscious users
- **Quick-win angle:** zero setup, works immediately on first visit
