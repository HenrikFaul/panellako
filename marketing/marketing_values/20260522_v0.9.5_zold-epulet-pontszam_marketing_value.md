# Marketing Value — Zöld Épület Pontszám (Green Building Score)

**Feature:** Feature 02 — Zöld Épület Pontszám  
**Version:** v0.9.5  
**Date:** 2026-05-22

---

## 1. One-sentence user-language description

PanelLakó instantly shows your building's 0–100 environmental score across air quality, green space, transport, cycling infrastructure, noise, and urban heat — all computed from live open data, so you know exactly how green your home really is.

---

## 2. Problem it replaces

Residents have no easy way to compare buildings on environmental factors. Prospective buyers and renters rely on subjective impressions or scattered government portals with no building-level aggregation. The manual process (checking OpenAQ, OSM maps, BKK stop lookups separately) takes hours and yields no single, comparable number.

---

## 3. Primary beneficiary personas

- **Conscious resident / prospective buyer** — wants objective proof that their building is in a healthy, sustainable neighbourhood
- **Building manager (közös képviselő)** — can benchmark and communicate the building's green credentials to residents
- **Environmentally-aware tenant** — uses the score when comparing apartments

---

## 4. Marketing-ready claim

"PanelLakó adja Magyarország első élőadatos Zöld Épület Pontszámát — 6 mért mutatóból, automatikusan, minden panelházra."

("PanelLakó delivers Hungary's first live-data Green Building Score — 6 measured dimensions, automatically, for every residential block.")

---

## 5. Claim types enabled

- **Transparency / visibility** — residents can see and share an objective environmental benchmark
- **Trust / authority** — live data from OpenAQ and OpenStreetMap, not estimates or surveys
- **Differentiation** — no comparable Hungarian residential platform offers this
- **Retention** — score freshness (1-hour cache + manual refresh) drives return visits

---

## 6. Content angles

1. **LinkedIn / Social:** "Milyen a te panelházad zöld pontszáma? PanelLakó most kiszámolja — 6 mutatóból, live adatból, ingyen." (What is your block's green score? PanelLakó computes it now — 6 metrics, live data, free.)
2. **SEO article:** "Hogyan mérhető egy lakóépület környezeti teljesítménye Budapesten?" — ranking for "zöld épület budapest" / "levegőminőség lakás budapest"
3. **Feature explainer post:** Behind-the-scores breakdown — what each of the 6 sub-scores means, why EU limits were chosen as references, what a Platina badge actually implies
4. **Case study hook:** Two buildings in the same street, different scores — why proximity to parks and distance from main roads matter more than building age
5. **Email campaign:** "Nézd meg az épületed Zöld Pontszámát" — engagement re-activation for dormant users

---

## 7. Funnel stage

- **Awareness** — shareable badge graphic ("Platina épületünk van!") drives organic social
- **Consideration** — prospective residents compare buildings during apartment search
- **Retention** — existing residents revisit dashboard monthly to track score changes
